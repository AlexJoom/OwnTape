OwnTape

Local recorder for Google Meet™

Allow your microphone

The Meet tab gives the voices of the other participants. To record your own voice too, the extension needs your microphone.

One click, then Chrome asks you

Click the button. Chrome shows a small window at the top: “Use your microphone?”. Click Allow. You do this one time only.

Look at the top of the window

Chrome asks for permission now. Click Allow.

Microphone allowed

Close this tab, go back to your Meet call, and click Start recording.

The microphone is blocked

Chrome blocks the microphone for OwnTape. Allow it in the Chrome settings, then try again.

  1. Click Open microphone settings.
  2. Next to Microphone, select Allow.
  3. Come back to this tab. OwnTape tries again by itself. If nothing happens, click Try again.

Do this when you do not record. The reload stops a recording that runs now.

  1. Click Open microphone settings, then click Reset permissions.
  2. Click Open the extensions page. Find OwnTape and click its reload icon (↻). This tab closes.
  3. Click the OwnTape icon in the toolbar and start again. When Chrome asks for the microphone, click Allow.

Also check that your operating system lets Chrome use the microphone. macOS: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. Windows: Settings › Privacy & security › Microphone. Linux: in your system sound settings (for example Settings › Sound › Input), select the correct input device and make sure that it is not muted. ChromeOS: Settings › Privacy and security › Privacy controls › Microphone access.

Or record without your voice: turn off Include my microphone in OwnTape.

What happens with your microphone

  • It is used only while you record, and only if “Include my microphone” is on.
  • The sound goes only into the video file on your computer. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • You can record without it. Then the file has the call audio, but not your voice.
