OwnTape

Local recorder for Google Meet™

Record your calls in three steps

Video and all voices go into one file on your computer. No account, no server, no tracking.

  1. 1

    Join your call

    Open your call on meet.google.com in Chrome, as usual.

  2. 2

    Click the icon

    Click the extension icon in the toolbar. Do not see it? Click the puzzle piece and pin it.

  3. 3

    Press Start

    Tell the participants that you record, then press Start recording. Press Stop to save the file.

Optional: include your own voice

The Meet tab gives the voices of the other participants. To record your voice too, allow the microphone once.

Allow microphone

Good to know

  • Recordings go to your Downloads folder as .webm video files. VLC, Chrome and most video players open them.
  • Keep the Meet tab open while you record. If you close it, the recording stops and the part so far is saved.
  • Nothing leaves your computer. The code is open: you can check it.
